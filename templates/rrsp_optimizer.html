RRSP deduction strategy optimizer

Compare three strategies for using an RRSP deduction over this year and next year:

  • Save the deduction and claim it all next year
  • Claim it fully now and invest the tax refund
  • Claim a partial amount now and the rest next year

The algorithm searches for the split that maximizes your wealth at the end of next year, assuming you invest any tax savings at your expected annual return.
